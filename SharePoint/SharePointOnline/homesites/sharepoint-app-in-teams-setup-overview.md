# Overview of how to plan, build, and launch the SharePoint app in Teams - SharePoint in Microsoft 365 | Microsoft Learn

The SharePoint app in Teams helps engage and empower different user groups like frontline workers and information workers across your organization. It helps keep them updated on news, announcements, and provides access to important resources from a desktop, mobile, or tablet device. Once established, end users can access these resources and information from within Microsoft Teams desktop, mobile, and web.

Note

- You must have an Enterprise (E), Frontline (F), or Academic (A) license type to create a SharePoint app in Teams experience.
- Users with a Microsoft 365 subscription (E, F, or A license) are limited to creating and using one experience. If you want to create or use two or more experiences (up to 50), then every user in your tenant must have a Microsoft Viva Suite or Viva Communications and Communities license. See [Microsoft Viva plans and pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-viva/pricing) for plan information and a comparison table.
- There are no requirements to get started with the SharePoint app in Teams.
- You must have SharePoint admin permissions to access the SharePoint admin center where you create and establish a communication site as a SharePoint home site.

Once you establish the SharePoint app in Teams, you work within the designated SharePoint site(s) to add and adjust content, assign permissions, designate targeted audiences, order the view priority of your experiences, and more.

- Use this guide to prepare your organization for the SharePoint app in Teams through the [planning](plan-sharepoint-app-in-teams), [building](build-sharepoint-app-in-teams), and [launching](launch-sharepoint-app-in-teams) phases.
- Review the different roles and activities in each phase. This will help determine if you need moer than one instance of the SharePoint app in Teams and the use of audience targeting throughout.
- Discover planning considerations and best practices.
- Get guidance for change management, adoption, and end-user training.

## Overview of activities in each phase

| Phase | Steps and tasks |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | **Step 1: Plan for the SharePoint app in Teams** Identify and align on key tasks and scenarios. **Step 2: Plan the dashboard** Learn more about the [different kinds of cards](available-dashboard-cards) and how they can support specific scenarios. **Step 3: Get ready for news publishing** Consider audience targeting to show news content to specific groups of people aggregated from various sites. **Step 4: Plan Resources** Determine which links belong in the [resources web part](https://support.microsoft.com/office/resources-web-part-6ab12c98-0249-4a3e-92d5-776ce2217d37). **Step 5: Create an adoption plan** Create a plan to help the rest of the organization adopt the ShaerPoint app in Teams. [Learn more about SharePoint app in Teams adoption](plan-sharepoint-app-in-teams#step-6-create-an-adoption-plan). **Step 6: Consider success metrics** Think about how your organization determines if the SharePoint app in Teams is meeting your specific needs. **Step 7: Plan for maintenance over time** Consider which content and components might need to be refreshed over time. |
| **Build** | **Step 1: [Create and designate home sites directly from the SharePoint admin center](/en-us/sharepoint/home-sites-improvements?source=docs#create-and-designate-home-sites-directly-from-the-sharepoint-admin-center)** Choose to create a single experience for the whole organization, or multiple experiences that service distinct audiences. **Step 2: Configure the SharePoint app in Teams** Design the overall experince across Microsoft Teams desktop and mobile from [within the SharePoint admin center](/en-us/viva/connections/sharepoint-teams-app-config). **Step 3: Pin the SharePoint app in Teams to the left side portion of Microsoft Teams** After the creation of your first experience, pin the app in the Teams admin center to make the SharePoint app in Teams easily discoverable. Navigate to your Teams admin center &gt; **Teams apps** &gt; **Setup policies**. Select **Global**. Scroll down to **Pinned apps**. Select **Add apps to add the SharePoint app in Teams**. If your [users have an F license, the SharePoint app in Teams is pinned by default](/en-us/microsoft-365/frontline/pin-teams-apps-based-on-license). **Step 4: Ensure high-traffic sites and portals are healthy** Run a health check and make sure everyone has access to the right content. **Step 5: Test and refine the experience** Find people in your organization who can test the experience before being released more broadly. |
| **Launch** | **Step 1: Consider tactics to spread the news** Think about where different audiences usually get news. Consider email campaign, outreach through SharePoint news and Teams posts, out to broader company Viva Engage communities, and specific training or All Hands meetings. **Step 2: Announce the SharePoint app in Teams** Use more than one communication channel to reach audiences. **Step 3: Use champions and provide continued support** Continue to use early adopters to help others get the most out of the SharePoint app in Teams and route feedback. Encourage organization leaders and stakeholders to notify others about the launch and progress each week, month, or quarter. |

## Overview of roles in each phase

| Phase | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | Work with **your organization’s stakeholders** and business partners (from groups like HR, operations, facilities, communications specialists, and technology advocates) to identify the most valuable content and tasks that can be completed in the SharePoint app in Teams. **Key administrative stakeholders typically include:** - **Business owners**, who lead efforts in different departments like sales, operations, design, engineering, etc.  - **HR professionals**, who manage team members and oversee company and organizational structure and efficiency.  - **Internal communication specialists**, who facilitate and manage communications between users and various internal teams.  - **Communications coordinators and specialists**, who execute and maintain company-wide communications and brand management.  - **Administrative assistants**, who complete and oversee various administrative tasks.  The more people that are informed, the more they will share progress and encourage others to participate. |
| **Build** | - The **SharePoint admin center admin** will set up the initial experience, add audiences, designate owners who can edit and customize each SharePoint app in Teams experience, and pins the app in Teams (if needed).  - **Site owners and authors for the SharePoint home site** design the site content, global navigation, and the dashboard.  - Other **site owners and authors** can be involved to update content or news posts that are relevant to the overall experience. |
| **Launch** | - Collaborate with **champions** (early adopters) to help other people at the organization implement the SharePoint app in Teams.  - Use **executive sponsors** who can help evangelize the app at large company meetings and in broad communications.  - **Business owners and managers** at all levels can help teams and individuals find value and adopt new ways of working.  Be open to feedback from all levels. It will be successful if everyone feels the SharePoint app in Teams was designed for them and maintained for them as business needs and culture change over time. |
